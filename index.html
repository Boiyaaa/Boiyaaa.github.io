My background is in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Auckland. I work on multimodal generation, with a particular interest in how AI can preserve character personality across visual stories.

My research explores personality-consistent visual storytelling and prototype-guided personality inference from text. During my internship at iFLYTEK’s Chongqing branch, I helped develop a novel analytics platform spanning data collection, sentiment analysis, and interactive visualization.

Trait Collapse in Text-to-Image Generation: Evaluating and Mitigating Personality Inconsistency in Visual Storytelling

Yuanbo Tong, Haoyuan Li, Jibiao Jiang, Zirui Wang, Yuchen Li, and Jiamou Liu

Identifies trait collapse: generated characters retain their appearance but lose personality-consistent behavior across scenes. Introduces ViStoryBench-Personality for evaluation and the training-free Trait–Behavior–Visual (TBV) framework, which translates personality traits into behavioral tendencies and renderable visual constraints.

Text-to-Image Personality Consistency Training-free
arXivPreprint

Cognitive alignment in personality reasoning: Leveraging prototype theory for MBTI inference

Haoyuan Li, Yuanbo Tong, Yuchen Li, Zirui Wang, Maoyuan Li, Zhongsheng Wang, Chunhou Liu, and Jiamou Liu

A prototype-guided framework for inferring MBTI from social-media text. Builds a balanced, quality-controlled prototype library and guides language models through a retrieve–reuse–revise–retain cycle, supporting interpretable personality predictions. Evaluated on the Kaggle and Pandora benchmarks.

MBTI Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning Prototype Learning
